Feel like home in Luanda

RK Suite Hotel offers accommodation with free private parking, as well as access to a fitness room and a terrace to enjoy a privileged view of the city of Luanda. Free WiFi is available throughout the property. Near the hotel there are several banks, convenience stores, pharmacy and easy access to transport.

We have a restaurant, common lobby, a meeting room and our staff speaks English and French.
