Luanda's Marginal

The Marginal de Luanda is a huge avenue that connects the Port of Luanda to the Island of Luanda, with a parallel boardwalk along the Bay of Luanda. This area of ​​Luanda has been completely renovated in recent years and is now one of the best places to walk and run in the city. From there you can see some of the best views of the city.
