The best places to visit in Luanda

Local Attractions The best places to visit in Luanda

Fort of S. Miguel

This fort is on top of a small hill next to the waterfront, so you have to climb some stairs to get there.

Find out more

Viewpoint of the Moon

The Miradouro da Lua is named after the lunar landscape that can be seen from the viewpoint.

Find out more

Quissama National Park

Quissama National Park is a great place for those who want to go on a Safari.

Find out more

Luanda island

It's not really an island but a small peninsula about 6 km long where the most famous bars, restaurants and beaches in Luanda are located.

Find out more

Mussulo Island

In the south of Luanda is located the island of Mussulo, with more than 30 km in length, thus forming a kind of giant maritime lagoon.

Find out more

Luanda's Marginal

The Marginal de Luanda is a huge avenue that connects the Port of Luanda to the Island of Luanda, with a parallel boardwalk along the Bay of Luanda.

Find out more

Coin Museum

It is a museum with some interactive exhibits and a lot of information about the history of the national currency.

Find out more
