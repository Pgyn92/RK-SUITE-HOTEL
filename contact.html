Your Hotel in Luanda city

Your Hotel in Luanda city

RK Suite Hotel is located in the heart of downtown Luanda, close to the main government offices, banks, oil companies and restaurants. Dr. António Agostinho Neto International Airport is approximately 42 km from the city centre — the journey takes around 40 to 60 minutes depending on traffic.

In case you have any doubt or need any help please feel free to call us and our front desk staff will be happy to help you.

How do I get to the hotel?

Dr. António Agostinho Neto International Airport (AIAAN, IATA: NBJ) is located approximately 42 km from downtown Luanda, in Icolo e Bengo municipality. Take the main expressway towards Luanda city centre (Baixa). Once in the city, head to Rua Rei Katyavala, Ed. Rei Katyavala — your driver or GPS will guide you easily.

We strongly recommend using Google Maps or the hotel's Plus Code: 56JV+7V Luanda. Our complimentary airport transfer is available upon prior request.
