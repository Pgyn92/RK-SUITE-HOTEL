Studio Suite

It is a super comfortable and cozy studio with a kitchen equipped with a balcony, a bathroom with a shower and free toiletries, and a living room with a sofa and TV. On the top floor is our gym and a balcony where you can enjoy a fantastic view of the city of Luanda. Even though it is an apartment, the RK Suite Hotel offers all hotel services such as laundry and room service if you don't want to go to our restaurant.
