All about us

All about us

RK Suite Hotel has the largest and more comfortable room in Luanda. With many years of experience our management team is prepared to meet the demands of business or leisure travelers, offering comfort and a permanent service with a timely response to all requirements.

Frequently asked questions

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation and prepayment policies vary depending on the type of accommodation.

What time is check-in and check-out?

At RK Suite Hotel, check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is until 12:00.

Do I need to book lunch or dinner?

It is not necessary. In the restaurant We have a buffet service for all meals.

Do you have laundry facilities?

Yes, we have

Do your rooms have cooking facilities?

Yes. If you don't want to eat in the restaurant or order room service, you can cook in your room

Do you have a club lounge, gym or pool?

Rk has only gym

Do you have room service?

Yes, we have room service available.

Can you smoke inside the rooms?

No, smoking is prohibited in all areas

Can I take a taxi from the hotel?

Yes, we have a partnership with a taxi company and we can schedule whenever you need.

Where is the closest airport?

Luanda International Airport

What kind of accommodation do you provide?

RK Suite Hotel offers 2 types of accommodation:

DELUXE SUITE

Apartment consists of a bedroom, equipped kitchen, bathroom with shower and free amenities, a common living and dining room. You can also enjoy 1 safe, TV, telephone, air conditioning, balcony to enjoy the city view, 1 work desk and Wi-Fi.

SUITE WITH BALCONY

It is a super comfortable and cozy studio with a kitchen equipped with a balcony, bathroom with shower and free toiletries, a living room with sofa and TV. You can also enjoy 1 safe, telephone, air conditioning, balcony to enjoy the city view, 1 work desk and Wi-Fi.
